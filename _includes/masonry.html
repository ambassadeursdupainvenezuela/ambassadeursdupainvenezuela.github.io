{%- comment -%} Shared masonry layout engine. Any container with `data-masonry-grid="true"` and class `masonry-grid` (items marked `masonry-grid-item`, see styles.html) gets its items' `grid-row-end` spans computed from their real rendered height, so items of different heights pack together without gaps. Works for any number of grids on the same page (e.g. a photo gallery and a blog cards grid can both live on index.html). If the grid is wrapped in `
` with a sibling `.masonry-skeleton` placeholder (see styles.html), this also reveals the real grid once it's correctly laid out and its images are loaded, crossfading from skeleton to real content instead of showing the grid mid-calculation. {%- endcomment -%}
